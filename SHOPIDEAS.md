

Ghostly Vapour

instead of dying you have 10 seconds as a ghost that can walk through walls to get back inside the kill boundary and return with 25% health. You cannot collect anything while being a vapour.

Big Sack

Sack can carry 10% more weight. Encumbrance increases when full.

Big Shoulders

Full beams
